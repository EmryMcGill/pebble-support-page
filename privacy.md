# Privacy Policy for Pebble

Last updated: Oct 18, 2025

Pebble ("the App") is a simple to-do list app that helps you organize tasks and categories. Your privacy is important, and this policy explains how your information is handled.

---

## Information We Collect

Pebble does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No account is required.
- No analytics or tracking is used.
- No data is sent to external servers.

All data created inside the app (tasks, categories, preferences) is stored **locally on your device only**.

---

## Third-Party Services

Pebble does not use any third-party services that collect user data.

---

## Data Security

Since all app data stays on your device, you are responsible for your device security. If you delete the app, your data will be permanently erased from your device.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Pebble is safe for all ages and does not collect any personal data from users of any age, including children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, it will be updated on this page.
